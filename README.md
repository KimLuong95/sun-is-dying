# Sun Is Dying

> A deep-future science-fiction series set ~5 billion years from now, as the Sun finally
> dies and the scattered branches of humanity gather to decide the fate of Earth — and,
> with it, who the legitimate heir of humanity really is.

**Status:** 🟡 *Pre-writing — agreeing the story spine before building out worldbuilding files.*

This repo is public **on purpose**: it exists so the author can share progress and spar with
AI collaborators (Claude, ChatGPT). It is a living design doc, not a finished manuscript.

---

## The one-line pitch
The Sun is dying, everyone knows it, and no one can stop it. That is only the clock. The real
story is that humanity has fractured into branches that no longer agree on *what a human is* —
and one family, the Keepers of a dying Earth, must decide its fate while being torn apart along
the exact same fault lines.

## Core questions
- **Cosmic:** If we are the only minds in a universe that may have been *made*, are we sacred,
  accidental, or meant to make the next one?
- **Personal:** When your family has become different kinds of being, who do you still owe your love to?

## Tone & comps
Epic scale of *Foundation* · religious depth of *Dune* · existential weight of *Children of Time*
— but with a **strong family-drama core**.

## Deliberately avoiding
- Simple good vs. evil (every branch is *right* from inside).
- Standard space empire.
- Alien invasion as the driver — the **absence** of aliens *is* the driver.

## How this repo is organized
The full worldbuilding will live in dedicated Markdown files, one fact/topic per file,
cross-linked. Planned structure (built out as the spine locks):

```
/STORY-SPINE.md        ← the agreed premise, decisions, and open questions  (start here)
/world/                ← universe, cosmology, deep-time, Earth, planets
/factions/             ← one file per branch of humanity
/religions/            ← belief systems (anchored on the origin mystery)
/characters/<name>/    ← bio + dated "growth" files as a character changes with age/events
/style/                ← prose voice, dialogue rules, POV conventions
/story/                ← series arc + per-book outlines
```

## Working with AI collaborators

**open**. When sparring with another AI, point it there. Open questions are flagged so a
collaborator can weigh in without re-deciding settled material.
