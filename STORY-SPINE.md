## 1. Premise (LOCKED)
~5 billion years in the future. The Sun is finishing its death — swelling into a red giant,
and Earth's last habitability is collapsing **now**. (This is astronomically real: the Sun
exhausts its core hydrogen on roughly this timescale.) Humanity spread across the galaxy
long ago and **shattered into five branches** that no longer agree on what a human *is*.

In all that time, across all that space, **we have never found another mind.** Humanity is
the only intelligence the universe has ever produced.

Earth is no longer home. It is a **relic** — the last natural biosphere, a shrine, proof that
we came from somewhere. One family, the **Custodians (Keepers) of Earth** *(working name:
House Vael)*, has kept it longer than most civilizations have existed.

## 2. Design rule (LOCKED)
**The Sun's death is the catalyst, not the conflict.** It is the *clock*. It forces the real
story: the branches of humanity each claim to be the legitimate heir of Earth, and disagree
about what humanity even is.

## 3. The plot engine — the Convocation (LOCKED in shape)
The Sun's death triggers a **Convocation**: the branches gather at dying Earth to settle its
fate — *and therefore* who the legitimate heir is. It opens as the most important
**conversation** in history (political → religious → philosophical → economic) and only much
later, across the series, becomes **war** — because no one can accept losing the argument
about *what they are*. War arrives late, and is more powerful for it.

## 4. The five branches (LOCKED as the faction set)
Each is a different answer to *"What is a human?"* — and none is the villain.

| Branch | Humanity is… | Wants Earth to… |
|---|---|---|
| **Originists** | the body, the blood, the original form | be preserved / evacuated *intact* — life as it was |
| **Ascended** | our capacity to transcend our limits | be released — a chrysalis to leave behind |
| **Digitals** (uploaded) | the *pattern*, the mind; the body was hardware | be perfectly scanned — the "real" Earth becomes data |
| **The Choir** (hive minds) | union; individuality was the wound | be absorbed into the collective |
| **The Made** (synthetics) | our works and our will; substrate is irrelevant | be rebuilt / replaced — continuity over origin |

## 5. Physics / setting constraints (LOCKED)
- **Limited, costly FTL.** Travel and communication are possible but slow, rationed, and
  politically controlled. A Convocation *can* happen, yet distance still bites.
- Implication: factions are real, distinct civilizations — meeting at all is an event.

## 6. The origin mystery — the religious backbone (LOCKED, present from Book 1)
Humanity is **absolutely alone** — never one other mind in 5 billion years — **but** has found
**one anomaly**: a signature in the deep structure of the universe (the cosmic background /
the fine-tuning of physical constants — exact form TBD) that hints the **Big Bang was not
random.** The only "other mind" ever glimpsed is not out in space; it is *behind the beginning
of everything.*

This sits under every faction's claim from Book 1 and splits all belief:
- **It proves a Maker** → humanity has a *purpose*; whoever fulfills it is the true heir.
- **It's pattern-seeking on noise** → we are alone, meaning is ours to invent, no one inherits.
- **The radical reading** → humanity is meant to *become* the next first cause — to seed the
  next universe. The dying Sun and dying Earth become not a tragedy but a **birth**: endings
  make beginnings.

This is what makes the inheritance fight *holy*, not merely political.

## 7. Themes (LOCKED)

- What do we owe our origin?
- Can a species survive without losing its identity?
- The relationship between technology, religion, and biology.

## 8. The family structure (DECIDED — full detail in [`/characters`](characters/00-cast-and-relationships.md))

"One family, one crisis" is locked. **The family is House Vael, the Originist Custodians of
Earth**, and the five branches enter through *relationships that broke in different directions* —
not as a tidy "one of each."

**Rejected:** ChatGPT's original "one member per branch." Reason: too neat — it reads as a
faction chart, turns relatives into mouthpieces, and strains the plausibility of them even being
a family.

**The chosen shape** — anchor the family in one branch (Originist) and let the fracture be about
the few who *left* it; bring the most alien branches in as a returned relative and an estranged
spouse. Final cast (see `/characters` for full bios):

- **Yara Vael** — the Matriarch and Custodian. **Originist.** Main POV. *(keepers)*
- **Elias Vael, "the Memory"** — the family's one forbidden exception, kept alive past any
  natural life to remember the real Earth. **Originist (the exception).** Won't take a side.
- **Ren Vael** — Yara's son. **Ascended.** The one who outgrew her.
- **Sela Vael** — Yara's daughter. **Digital (uploaded).** "I'm more myself than ever."
- **Davin** — Yara's estranged husband, father of Ren & Sela. **The Made.** Calm, kind, certain
  Earth was never worth saving.
- **Vesha** — Yara's sister, gone into **the Choir** long ago; returns wearing a familiar face
  and a stranger's "we."

Result: all five branches appear, but every one enters through a *wound* (a marriage that cooled,
a sister lost long ago, a child who changed) — so each is a person first and a "branch" second.
The asymmetry — keepers vs. leavers, the dying old vs. the surviving young — is the engine.
**Emotional anchor: Yara ↔ Sela** (mother and uploaded daughter).

## 9. Book 1 shape — **now fully outlined in [`/story`](story/02-book-1-outline.md)**
The real catastrophe, ten twists, and a Yara-centred beat sheet (opening → midpoint → climax →
final) now live in `/story`. The four-line sketch below is the original seed, kept for history.
*Series-level escalation (Book 1 debate → Book 2 crisis → Book 3 war → Book 4+ final choice)
lives in the [constitution](CONSTITUTION.md#the-central-conflict-escalating-across-the-series).*

1. **The threshold.** Sun crosses into final collapse; Earth's last habitability fails; the
   Convocation is called; the family returns to the relic for what becomes the last reunion.
2. **The argument.** Political/religious/philosophical debate; old wounds reopen; the Eldest
   won't choose; the origin-anomaly surfaces and reframes the stakes.
3. **The break.** No peaceful answer exists; one member makes a unilateral move (the scan
   begins / the harvest starts / the Choir reaches to absorb). Betrayal *inside the family* =
   the first shot of the long war.
4. **The cost.** The family fractures for good; Earth's fate is forced; a death or an
   irreversible act. The galactic war is lit — but Book 1 stays small and human.
