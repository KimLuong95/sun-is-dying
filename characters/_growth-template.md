# Growth File — Template & Rules

Copy this into `characters/<name>/growth/NN-short-title.md` whenever a character goes through a
change big enough to make them a different person: an age crossed, a loss, a betrayal, a choice
they can't take back.

The point of these files is to make characters feel **real** — people are the sum of what
happened to them. A reader should never feel a character "suddenly" acts differently. We should
always be able to trace it here.

---

## Title — *(what changed, in a few words)*
**When:** *(their age / where in the story / how long before "now")*
**One line:** *(the change in a sentence)*

### Before
Who they were going in. What they believed, wanted, feared. Be specific — name the thing they'd
have said out loud and the thing they wouldn't.

### The moment
What actually happened. Keep it concrete — an event, not a mood. The smallest true detail
(a word said, an object, a silence) is worth more than a summary.

### After
What's different now — in what they want, how they speak, what they avoid, what they can no
longer feel or can suddenly feel too much. Note any new habit, scar, tic, or phrase that shows
the change *without anyone explaining it*.

### Cost
What it took from them. Every real change costs something. If it didn't, it wasn't a change.

### Threads
Anything this opens for later — a wound waiting to reopen, a debt, a lie now in place.

---

> **Rule of thumb:** if you can't fill in *Cost*, it's not a growth beat — it's just a mood, and
> it doesn't get a file.
