# Elias Vael — The Memory

**Branch:** Originist (the family's one forbidden exception) · **Role:** the living memory of
Earth · **POV:** rarely (used sparingly, for weight)

## Snapshot
The family calls him Grandfather. No one knows how many generations that's actually true across,
or how old he really is — the number is lost, and he doesn't offer it. Elias is the oldest living
mind any of them has ever met: a man kept alive far, far past a natural life so that *someone*
would still remember the real Earth, from back when it was green and full and the Sun was kind.

He is the family's deepest secret. The Vaels, who believe above all in *not changing*, changed
the rules exactly once — to keep him. He is living proof that even the keepers cheated. That
shames them, and they love him anyway.

## Personality
Gentle, unhurried, and a little far away, like someone listening to music no one else can hear.
He has outlived so much that grief and joy have worn down into the same soft thing in him. He
isn't wise in a grand way — he's wise in the way of a person who has simply seen the same human
heart make the same choices ten thousand times.

## How he talks
Slowly. In small, concrete pictures from a world that no longer exists — *"the smell of rain on
hot stone," "a kind of bird that is gone now."* He answers big questions with tiny memories. He
forgets names, including sometimes his own, but never the *feeling* of a thing.

## Want vs. need
- **Wants:** only one thing — that Earth, and what it truly was, not be *forgotten*. Not saved as
  rock, not scanned as data. Remembered. Felt.
- **Needs:** to finally be allowed to rest — and to decide whether his last act is to hand his
  memory on, or take it with him.

## Why he won't take a side
Elias remembers a time before the branches, when humanity was simply one thing. To him, every
side at the Convocation is a piece of the same lost child arguing with itself. He can't bring
himself to call any of them wrong — or any of them right. This neutrality makes him the moral
center of the book and the prize everyone wants to claim.

## Key relationships
- **Yara** — she wants him as an ally; he gives her love instead of agreement, which infuriates
  and steadies her in equal measure.
- **All five branches** — each wants the Memory to bless *their* claim. Whoever Elias seems to
  lean toward, the others fear.

## Arc seed (Book 1)
The man who refuses to choose is slowly cornered by a world where everyone must. His final
choice — what to do with the only first-hand memory of Earth left in existence — may matter more
than the Convocation's official decision.
