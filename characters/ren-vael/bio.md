# Ren Vael — The One Who Outgrew Her

**Branch:** Ascended · **Role:** Yara's son · **POV:** yes (secondary)

## Snapshot
Ren left home to become more than he was born as, and it worked. He is faster, sharper, calmer,
and longer-lived than his mother will ever be — and he loves her too much to say out loud that he
thinks her whole life is a beautiful mistake. He came back for the Convocation telling himself
it's duty. Really, he came back because some part of him still wants her to say she's proud.

## Personality
Brilliant, charming, and just a little tired of everyone else being slow. Ren has the easy
confidence of a person who upgraded past most of his fears — which means the few he has left run
very deep. He's not cold. He's the opposite: he feels everything, fast, and has trained himself
to act before the feeling can slow him down.

## How he talks
Quick, warm, a touch too clever — he wins arguments he should lose just by talking faster. He
hides tenderness inside jokes. When he's truly hurt, the speed drops out of his voice and he goes
very quiet, which is the only reliable sign that something has actually landed.

## Want vs. need
- **Wants:** to drag his family *forward* — to make them survive by changing, the way he did.
- **Needs:** to understand that "saving" people by making them more like you isn't love — and
  that his mother's refusal to change is not weakness, but a different kind of courage.

## The flaw
He confuses *improvement* with *worth*. Deep down he believes the version of you that survives is
the better one — which means he half-believes his mother, and the whole Originist way, deserves to
lose.

## Where he stands on Earth
**Let it go, and take the family with him.** Ren finds the fight to preserve Earth almost unbearable
to watch — not because he doesn't care, but because he cares about *them*, and they're spending
their last strength on a rock instead of on surviving.

## Key relationships
- **Yara** — the approval he'll never quite get and can't stop chasing.
- **Sela** — his sister; the one person who out-radicalized him. He's quietly unsettled that she
  went further than he ever would.
- **Davin** — his father, the Made; the version of "changing yourself" taken to the end. Ren
  looks at Davin and sees either his future or his warning, and doesn't know which.

## Arc seed (Book 1)
From *certain* to *afraid*. Ren starts sure that change is salvation. Watching his father's
cold, kind machine-logic — and his sister's bottomless calm — he begins to wonder where the
"improving" stops, and whether there's a version of *more* that has nothing human left in it.
