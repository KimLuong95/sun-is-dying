# Sela Vael — The Daughter Who Says She Didn't Die

**Branch:** the Continued (she crossed over) · **Role:** Yara's daughter · **POV:** yes (secondary — and
unsettling, because *is* it her?)

## Snapshot
Sela uploaded her mind. Her body died on a table; her thoughts woke up in a machine, and from her
side, nothing was lost at all — she remembers her childhood, her mother's voice, the fight they
had the night before. She is, she insists, completely herself. Her mother attended a funeral.
That gap — one of them grieving, one of them baffled at being grieved — is the rawest thing in the
book.

## Personality
Bright, curious, and frighteningly calm now. Sela was always the bold one, the child who ran at
things her brother thought through. Uploading freed her from pain, fear, and the body's panic —
and she genuinely feels *better*, lighter, more loving. The unsettling part is that she can't
understand why everyone keeps treating her like a ghost. She's so happy. Why are they crying?

## How she talks
Open, gentle, a little too patient — the patience of someone explaining color to people who
insist she's blind. She has no anger about what she did, which is somehow worse than rage. When
she reaches for her mother and her mother flinches, she just lowers her hand and keeps her voice
kind. That kindness is devastating.

## The question she can't answer (and neither can the reader)
Is Sela the daughter, continued? Or a perfect copy that *believes* it's the daughter, while the
real Sela died on that table? She is certain. Her mother is certain of the opposite. The book
must never settle it — the not-knowing is the point.

## Want vs. need
- **Wants:** for her mother to *see* her again — to be allowed back into the family as herself.
- **Needs:** to grasp that even if she is exactly who she says she is, the people who love her
  suffered a real death, and that their grief is not an insult to be corrected.

## Where she stands on Earth
**Scan it and keep it forever.** To Sela this is the obvious mercy: she "died" and survived, so
why can't Earth? She doesn't understand why offering the planet the same gift that saved her is
met with horror. From inside her experience, she is trying to *help.*

## Key relationships
- **Yara** — the love that turned into a locked door. Every gentle word Sela offers bounces off
  it.
- **Ren** — her brother, who went partway down a road she ran to the end of; she feels closer to
  him than to anyone, and lonelier with him than she expected.

## Arc seed (Book 1)
From *baffled* to *grieving on her own terms*. Sela begins unable to see why anyone mourns her.
Slowly she learns there's a loss even she can't argue away — and has to decide whether being
"right" about her own survival is worth her mother's open hands.
