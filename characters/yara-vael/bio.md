# Yara Vael — The Custodian

**Branch:** Originist · **Role:** Matriarch of House Vael, Keeper of Earth · **POV:** yes (main)

## Snapshot
Yara is the woman who holds the dying world. By blood and by oath she is the Custodian of Earth —
the latest in a line older than most civilizations — and she carries it the way you carry
something you love and can't save: carefully, and with a kind of dread. She is not a queen or a
general. She's a caretaker who has run out of time.

## Personality
Steady on the outside, cracking underneath. Yara is practical, patient, and used to being the one
who stays calm while others fall apart — because someone has to. She mistakes endurance for
strength and silence for control. She would rather carry a thing alone than admit it's too heavy.
Her warmth is real but rationed; she learned long ago that the Custodian doesn't get to need
things.

## How she talks
Plain and quiet. Short sentences when she's hurt, which is often. She deflects big feelings with
small practical questions — *"Have you eaten?"* in the middle of an argument that's breaking her
heart. She almost never says what she actually means the first time.

## Want vs. need
- **Wants:** to save Earth — and, underneath that, to keep her family from scattering into things
  she can't recognize.
- **Needs:** to learn that holding on and loving are not the same thing — and that she may have
  to let go of the world, or her children, or both, without it meaning she failed them.

## The flaw
She believes that if she just holds tightly enough, nothing will be lost. So she grips — and the
gripping is exactly what drives them away. **Where it was learned:** Yara was never meant to be
Custodian — her elder sister Vesha was the heir, and walked into the Choir. The first thing Yara
ever held was a post someone had let go of. (See [the joining](../vesha-choir/growth/01-the-joining.md).)

## Where she stands on Earth
**Save it as it is, whatever it costs.** To Yara this isn't politics or even faith — it's the one
promise her whole life was built around. Letting Earth die feels like letting her ancestors,
her children, and her own meaning die at once.

## Key relationships
- **Sela** (daughter, uploaded) — the wound that won't close. Yara held her child's cooling body
  and now refuses to call the thing on the screen by her daughter's name.
- **Ren** (son, Ascended) — he didn't die, he *outgrew* her, and somehow that's worse.
- **Davin** (husband, now Made) — the love that didn't end in a fight, just in distance she can't
  cross.
- **Vesha** (sister, in the Choir) — the first person she ever lost this way; the original grief.
- **Elias** ("Grandfather," the Memory) — the one who should be on her side and won't be.

## Arc seed (Book 1)
From *gripping* to *choosing*. Yara begins certain that saving Earth and saving her family are
the same act. The Convocation slowly forces her to see they might be opposites — and that being a
good Custodian might mean finally opening her hands.
