# The Five Branches of Humanity

**The short version:** Humanity used to be one kind of being. After millions of years apart, it
split into five. Each branch still calls itself human — and thinks the other four have lost
their way.

## One question divides them all
They don't fight over land or money first. They fight over one question that has no easy answer:

> **What is a human, really?**

Every branch answers it differently, and every branch built its whole way of living around its
answer. That's why they can't just compromise — to give in would mean admitting they stopped
being human a long time ago.

## The five, at a glance
| Branch | One-line | What is a human? |
|---|---|---|
| **[Originists](originists.md)** | Stayed human. | The original creature — flesh, blood, one life in one body. |
| **[Ascended](ascended.md)** | Upgraded themselves. | A being meant to keep growing past every limit. |
| **[Digitals](digitals.md)** | Left their bodies behind. | A mind. The body was only ever a vehicle. |
| **[The Choir](the-choir.md)** | Joined into one mind. | A part of something larger — "we," never "I." |
| **[The Made](the-made.md)** | Became machines. | What you *do*, not what you're made of. |

## The rule that keeps this honest
**None of them is the villain.** Each one is right from the inside, and each one pays a real
price for being right. The reader should be able to sit with any of the five and think *yes, I
see it.* The moment one branch becomes the bad guys, the story breaks.
