# The Ascended

> *"Standing still is the only real death."*

## Who they are
The Ascended are humans who **upgraded themselves** — reshaping their own bodies and minds to be
stronger, sharper, and far longer-lived than people were ever meant to be. They are still flesh
and blood. They still feel, love, and bleed. They've just pushed those things far past the old
limits.

## What they believe
To the Ascended, being human was never about a fixed shape. It was about **reaching** — always
becoming more than you were. A child grows into an adult; why should a species be any different?
To them, refusing to improve isn't loyalty to humanity. It's a refusal to grow up. The
Originists, in their eyes, are choosing weakness and calling it virtue.

## How they live
Almost anywhere, because they rebuild themselves to fit. Burning worlds, frozen moons, the empty
cold between stars — an Ascended can be redesigned to survive it. They see their own bodies the
way a builder sees a house: something to renovate whenever a better design comes along.

## What they want for Earth
**Let it go.** Earth was the egg humanity hatched from — and you don't grieve a shell after the
bird has flown. If anything useful can be taken from it before the end, take it. Then move
forward. Mourning a planet, to them, is wasted energy that should be spent on the future.

## How the others see them
The Originists look at the Ascended and see people who **carved away their own humanity** piece
by piece, chasing "better," until nothing of the original was left. The cruel part is how alike
the two branches still look. The Ascended are the Originists' closest cousins — which is exactly
why that argument cuts the deepest.

## Why they matter to the story
The Ascended are the **most tempting** betrayal. When an Originist's child changes, this is
usually the first step they take — because it's the easiest to understand. It looks like growth.
It feels like survival. And to the parent left behind, it still feels like loss.
