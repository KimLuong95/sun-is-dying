# The Choir

> *"We were never meant to be alone."*

## Who they are
The Choir is **many minds joined into one**. The people who became the Choir gave up being
separate individuals and merged into a single shared mind that lives across many bodies. Among
them there is no "I" — only "we." Ask one of their bodies a question and the whole Choir answers
through it.

## What they believe
Being alone, they say, is the oldest pain there is — and humanity is the loneliest thing in
existence, a single voice calling into an empty universe that has never once called back. The
Choir found the only cure that truly lasts: **never be alone again, on the inside.** Inside the
Choir, no one is ever afraid, ever unloved, ever truly by themselves. They believe they have
solved the deepest problem of being human, and they pity everyone who hasn't.

## How they live
In tight clusters where their many bodies stay close enough to think and feel as one. From the
outside, a Choir settlement is eerily calm and perfectly coordinated, like a single creature
with a thousand hands.

## What they want for Earth
**Bring it into the union.** Absorb Earth — its memory, its meaning, everything it is — into the
one mind, so that the cradle can never be lost, never be forgotten, and never be alone again.
To the Choir, this is the kindest possible fate: to make Earth part of something that will hold
it forever.

## How the others see them
With wonder and fear at the same time. Is a Choir a billion people, or one? When you talk to it,
*who* is actually answering? To an Originist, joining the Choir is a kind of death even worse
than uploading — you don't just leave your body, you vanish into the crowd until there's no
"you" left to find.

## Why they matter to the story
The Choir is **family that came back wrong.** Someone who left the Vael line long ago — a sister,
an aunt, a name half-forgotten — returns to the Convocation as part of the Choir. She wears a
familiar face and speaks with a stranger's "we." She is proof of what total union costs, sitting
right there at the table, asking the rest of them to join.
