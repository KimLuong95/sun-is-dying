# The Makers' Faith

> *"We were not an accident. We were called."*

## What they believe
The clue in the universe — the pattern that looks too neat to be random — is, to the Makers'
Faith, the **fingerprint of a creator.** Something made the universe on purpose, and made it in
a way that would lead, eventually, to minds like ours. We are not a fluke. We are the point.

And if we were made on purpose, then we have a **purpose** — a task we were created to carry out.
The faithful don't all agree on what that task is, but they agree completely that it exists, and
that finding it is the most important thing any human can do.

## Why it's powerful
It answers the loneliest question with the warmest possible reply: *you are not alone, and you
were wanted.* In a dark, empty universe, that comfort is overwhelming. It gives suffering a
reason and death a meaning. People will follow that feeling anywhere.

## How it shows up in the conflict
This is the most common faith, and the most dangerous, because **whoever can claim to be
fulfilling the creator's purpose can claim to be the true heir of humanity.** Every branch wants
that crown. Expect each of them to argue that *their* way of being — staying human, ascending,
uploading, joining, rebuilding — is what we were *made* for.

## The shadow side
A purpose handed down from a creator is not something you can argue with. The most extreme
believers decide that anyone standing in the way of humanity's purpose is standing in the way of
*God* — and that's the kind of certainty that starts wars. The Makers' Faith can be the gentlest
comfort in the book and the spark that lights the worst of it.
