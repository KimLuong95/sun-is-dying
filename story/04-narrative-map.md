# Narrative Map — What Each Stretch of the Book Must Accomplish

> The outline ([02](02-book-1-outline.md)) says what *happens*. This map says what the **reader**
> must know, feel, and wonder by the end of each movement — so the book reads as one story, not a
> string of well-written but disconnected chapters. **A chapter that's beautiful but doesn't move
> its movement's goal is a failure, and gets fixed.**

The companion discipline: after drafting any chapter, update the
[reader-knowledge ledger](05-reader-knowledge-ledger.md) — if the chapter didn't deliver its
KNOW-items plainly enough for a tired reader on a phone, it isn't done.

---

## Movement I — The Gathering (Ch. 1–5) ✅ written
**Goal in one line:** meet the family, the dying world, and the Stone — and watch one reflex
start a war.

**By the end, the reader must KNOW (plainly):**
- The Sun is dying; Earth has years, not centuries. Everyone always knew; the *speed* is new.
- Humanity became **five kinds** that disagree about what a human is.
- One family (Vael) keeps Earth; Yara is the Custodian; her family spans the divide.
- The First Stone = the heirship of humanity; only the recognized heir may lift it; no one ever has.
- Yara lifted it and held on. The law read that as a claim. The race for Earth and the Stone has begun.
- The Made keep Earth's sky alive (the lattice) — and showed everyone what that means.
- A sixth claim exists: the Faithful Dead, who want Earth *un*-saved.

**Must FEEL:** the Yara↔Sela wound; the dread of good manners over an abyss.
**Must WONDER:** is Sela really Sela? What did Elias mean by "another one"? What will Yara do
with the Stone? Why hasn't Sela come back?

## Movement II — The Race Begins Politely (Ch. 6–10)
**Goal:** the hidden stake appears, and Yara stops reacting.
**KNOW:** there is a strange signal in the universe's deep structure (the anomaly) and Earth may
be the last place it can be read; the Made's lattice triage forces every faction's plan onto a
clock; Yara makes her own irreversible move (end of movement).
**FEEL:** Yara needing the daughter she buried; the family pulling apart under courtesy.
**WONDER:** what is the anomaly? What did the keepers before Yara know? What is Ren hiding?

## Movement III — The Mirror (Ch. 11–16) — *midpoint movement*
**Goal:** the cosmic stake lands and flips the board.
**KNOW:** the anomaly hints the universe's beginning was *meant*; Earth is the last window to
even ask; the window dies with the Sun; every faith's claim now runs through Earth.
**FEEL:** Yara's certainty cracking — she must trust "the copy" with the most important thing in
history.
**WONDER:** were we made? Is the mirror showing truth or our own faces? (Never answered — Rule 7.)

## Movement IV — Pulled Apart (Ch. 17–22)
**Goal:** every love and every faith becomes a lever; trust rots.
**KNOW:** the five plans are mutually exclusive and the first mover wins reality; the Faithful
Dead have become a movement; someone is preparing to move.
**FEEL:** Yara lying to people she loves; the cost of holding on.
**WONDER:** who moves first? What is Elias not saying? (Seeds: the cradle-restoration, the old
schism.)

## Movement V — The Break (Ch. 23–26)
**Goal:** the first irreversible act — by family.
**KNOW:** who moved and why they believed it was love; what it destroyed.
**FEEL:** the loss (a death or equivalent that costs the reader).
**WONDER:** can anything be kept now?

## Movement VI — Open Hands (Ch. 27–30)
**Goal:** want vs. need resolves; the answer answers back.
**KNOW:** what Yara chose, what it cost, what Earth's fate now is (in motion, not finished).
**FEEL:** open hands — grief that is finally honest.
**WONDER (into Book 2):** the anomaly *responded*. What noticed? What did it say?

---

## The synergy check (run at every movement boundary)
1. Read the movement's chapters in one sitting. Does a first-time reader, asked "what is
   happening and what's at stake?", answer correctly in two sentences?
2. Check the movement's KNOW-list against the [ledger](05-reader-knowledge-ledger.md): every
   item delivered **on the page, plainly, at least once** — epigraphs count, narration hints
   alone don't.
3. Check the WONDER-list: every open question deliberately planted, none accidentally answered.
4. Then run the chapter-level [revision pass](../style/05-the-revision-pass.md) as usual.
