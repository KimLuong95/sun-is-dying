# How Humanity Is Spread Out (and Why No One Is in Charge)

**The short version:** Space is huge and crossing it is slow and costly, so humanity grew apart
into separate peoples. No one rules everything. They only gather when something forces them to.

## Travel is possible — but hard
Humans *can* cross from star to star faster than light. But it's not easy: a trip still takes
years, it's dangerous, and it costs a fortune. Sending a message across that distance is slow
too. So almost no one travels far very often.

Think of the galaxy as a vast ocean dotted with islands. Boats exist — but the crossings are so
long and expensive that most people never leave their own shore.

## Why humanity grew apart
Because the different groups of humanity were cut off from each other for millions of years,
they slowly drifted into almost separate species — not just in how they look, but in what they
believe and how they live. Each group changed in its own direction until they could barely
recognize one another.

That's how one kind of human became **five very different branches.** (See [the five branches](../factions/00-overview.md).)

## No one is in charge
There is no emperor, no government, no council that rules all of humanity. Nothing like that
could ever hold together across such distance and such difference. Humanity is a **patchwork** —
many separate peoples who mostly leave each other alone, partly by choice and partly because
reaching each other is so hard.

This matters: when a big decision has to be made, there is no one with the power to *make*
everyone obey. Agreement has to be earned, not ordered.

## The Convocation
Every so often — very, very rarely — the branches each send representatives to one place to
settle something that affects them all. This gathering is called a **Convocation.**

It's fragile by nature. No one can force anyone to honor what's decided. It works only as long
as everyone shows up willing to listen. The death of Earth has called the largest, tensest
Convocation anyone can remember — and that meeting is where the story begins.
