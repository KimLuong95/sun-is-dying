# Names & Language — Identity You Can Hear

**The short version:** In this world a name tells you, instantly, **how far a person stands from
the human they started as.** Naming is worldbuilding. Use it on purpose.

When the reader meets someone, the *shape* of the name should already whisper which branch they
belong to and how much of the old human is left in them — before they say a word.

## The rule we never break
**No apostrophe-soup.** No `Xy'rahn`, `K'thal`, `Zypheros`, `Vrax'thul`. That's the trap that
makes everything sound like generic sci-fi. Names must be **pronounceable** and feel **historical,
not random** — the way *Dune* gets it right (Leto, Chani, Gurney) by making names strange but
human-rooted. If you can't say it out loud easily, change it.

## The three tiers (a spectrum from rooted to alien)

### Tier 1 — Kept names (the Originists)
Recognizably human names, the kind we'd know today, sometimes lightly worn by age:
**David. Sarah. Fatima. Michael. Yara. Elias. Lyra.**

For Originists this is not an accident — it's **devotion.** To keep an old human name across
five billion years is an act of faith, a refusal to drift. The [Cradle](../religions/the-cradle.md)
faithful treat a birth-name as sacred inheritance, handed down the long chain from the first
people. A kept name says: *I am still one of the originals, and proud of it.*

### Tier 2 — Drifted names (those who changed but remember)
Old names worn down and reshaped by unimaginable spans of time and language drift:
- **David → Davin → Daev → Daviir**
- **Sarah → Sael → Saira**
- **Michael → Mikha → Kael**

A drifted name signals someone — or a whole culture — that moved away from baseline humanity but
still carries an echo of where it came from. The Ascended often bear these: human enough to
remember, changed enough to show it.

### Tier 3 — Functional & chosen names (the most post-human)
Names that are really **titles or descriptions** — the mark of a mind that may no longer think
the way we do at all:
**Sol Witness. Memory-of-Dawn. Last Son of Earth. Continuity-72. Ninth Echo. The Concord.**

When a character is named like this, the reader should feel a small chill: *this one might not
be a person in the way I understand the word.* Common among Digitals, the Made, and the Choir.

## How it maps to the branches
| Branch | Typical naming | What the name signals |
|---|---|---|
| **Originists** | Tier 1 — kept human names | "I never left. I am the root." |
| **Ascended** | Tier 2 — drifted, often aspirational | "I grew past what I was, but I remember it." |
| **Digitals** | Tier 2 or 3 — birth-name kept *or* a chosen handle | depends how far they've let the body go |
| **The Choir** | Tier 3 / none — plural or functional | "There is no single 'I' here to name." |
| **The Made** | Tier 3 — functional designations | "Origin is a footnote. I am what I do." |

## House / family names
Lineage names drift too. Over deep time a surname wears smooth into something archaic and
pronounceable — like **Vael**, the Custodian family's name: old, eroded, and impossible to date.
Surnames carry the weight of *how long* a line has lasted.

## How to name a new character (the recipe)
1. **How far from baseline are they?** (rooted → changed → post-human)
2. **What do they believe about origin?** (devout keepers cling to old names; the Made discard them)
3. **Pick the tier**, then say it out loud. If it's a mouthful or sounds random, fix it.

## Our cast, decoded
- **Yara Vael** — Tier 1. A real, kept human name. She *is* the root. *(Vael = ancient eroded house name.)*
- **Elias Vael** — Tier 1, deliberately archaic. A name that "survived" — fitting, since he's the
  one who survived to remember.
- **Ren Vael** — Tier 2, contracted. A human name worn short by his ascension; an echo, not the
  whole word.
- **Sela Vael** — Tier 1, kept. Born Originist, *uploaded later* — so she still carries her human
  birth-name, which is exactly why losing her hurts: she's still got her little-girl name.
- **Davin** — Tier 2, **drifted from an Originist birth-name (a "David").** Among the Made he holds
  a cold functional designation; to his family he is still the worn echo "Davin." That gap is the
  whole tragedy of him. *(He dropped the house name "Vael" when he left — another wound.)*
- **Vesha** — Tier 1 birth-name, but the Choir has **no personal name** for itself. It wears
  "Vesha" only to reach Yara. When it speaks of itself, it says "we," or gives a functional
  Concord-designation. The name is bait made of love.
